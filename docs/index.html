Length-frequency simulation

1. Simulating length-frequency data: a step-by-step guideline (single-sex stock)

North Sea plaice (ple4, ple4.index): growth from catch weights, inverse ALKs, length selectivity for a commercial fleet and a survey (build_gear()), catch partition by gear, single- and two-stage sampling (len.sim(), lfd.sim()), effective sample size and common random numbers, age-length keys, raising to catch weight. Then a January pulse survey with continuous (quarterly, monthly) fishery sampling, the season switch, and annual versus continuous designs contrasted. Finally both designs in an mse::mp() run with sampling.lfd.oem() and checks.

2. Sex-structured stocks (unit.type = "gender")

In preparation. ple4sex with sex-specific growth, one sampling event per gear on the combined catch, sexed and unsexed length samples.

3. Spatial stocks (unit.type = "spatial")

In preparation. Areas as units with area-specific gears, independent samples per area, raising to catch by area and pooling. Supporting material for the ICES deep-sea working group report.
